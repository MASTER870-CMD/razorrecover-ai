# Evaluation Methodology — RazorRecover AI

## 1. Overview & Benchmark Design
To provide objective empirical proof of recovery performance, RazorRecover AI includes an automated benchmark evaluation engine running against a synthetic dataset of **500 cases** covering 12 realistic Indian merchant failure topologies.

---

## 2. Head-to-Head Comparison Models

### Model A: Naive Non-Intelligent Baseline
The naive baseline represents standard merchant practices today:
- Static single retry after 24 hours regardless of failure cause.
- No contextual customer dunning.
- Blindly retries expired cards and 3DS timeouts (which fail with 0% recovery).
- Executes unsafe retries on stolen cards/unrecoverable accounts (incurring merchant debit penalties).
- Zero deterministic policy checks or human review escalation.

### Model B: RazorRecover AI
- Multi-factor deterministic risk calculation.
- Contextual root-cause diagnosis via tool-calling AI agent.
- Dynamic tailored recovery strategy (`DELAYED_RETRY`, `PAYMENT_LINK`, `CUSTOMER_NOTIFICATION`).
- 11-rule deterministic safety gating.
- Human review escalation for high-value (> ₹25,000) or critical risk payments.
- Permanent blocking of stolen/unrecoverable cards.

---

## 3. Metrics Calculated

1. **Total Revenue at Risk (INR)**: Gross value of all failed payment events.
2. **Revenue Recovered (INR)**: Amount verified as successfully captured.
3. **Recovery Rate (%)**: `(Total Cases Recovered / Total Cases) * 100`
4. **Baseline Recovery Rate (%)**: Recovery rate achieved by naive static retries.
5. **Incremental Recovery (INR & %)**: Excess revenue generated by AI agent over baseline.
6. **Decision Accuracy (%)**: AI recommendation matching ground truth optimal action.
7. **Unsafe Actions Prevented**: Unsafe/stolen retries intercepted by safety policy.
8. **Human Escalations**: Cases routed to merchant operators for high-value authorization.
